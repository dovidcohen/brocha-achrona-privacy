Privacy Policy

Brocha Achrona · Last updated: October 28, 2026

Brocha Achrona ("the app") reminds you to say a brocha achrona after eating. It is designed with your privacy as a priority. This policy explains how the app handles your information, on the phone (iOS and Android) and on the watch (Apple Watch and Wear OS).

We do not collect your data

Brocha Achrona does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no server, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Location

If you turn on the alert for leaving the place where you started eating, the app uses your location while a session is open, only to notice that you have moved away. The starting point is kept on your phone until the session ends and is then discarded. It is never sent anywhere and is not stored in your history.

The app also uses your phone's last known location to work out the time of sunset, so that it can tell which Hebrew date a meal belongs to (for Rosh Chodesh, Chanukah and similar days). This calculation happens on your phone, and the location is not sent anywhere.

Your history stays on your device

Finished sessions (which brochos, when, and whether you said them) and your settings are saved locally on your phone so you can look back at them. You can delete your history at any time from the History screen, and it is removed if you delete the app.

The watch app

If you use the watch app, your phone and watch exchange the current session and your settings directly, over the link your devices already use with each other (Apple's paired-watch connection, or Google's Wear OS connection). Nothing passes through a server of ours, and the watch keeps only what it needs to show the current session.

Notifications

All reminders and alerts are created on your device. Nothing is pushed from a server.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
